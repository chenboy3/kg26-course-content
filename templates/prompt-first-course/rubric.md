# Prompt-first lab rubric

Score the reasoning and communication shown in the submitted artifacts.

| Area | Weight | What good work shows |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Question and decision framing | 25% | A specific audience, decision, and testable question |
| Evidence and data quality | 25% | Relevant tables or charts plus missingness, units, and suspicious-value checks |
| Interpretation | 20% | Clear separation between observed facts and inferred explanations |
| Model judgment | 20% | A baseline comparison, honest error analysis, and a useful statement of limits |
| Communication and revision | 10% | Concise findings that changed when evidence challenged the first idea |

Do not score code volume. A student can earn full credit through strong prompts,
careful evidence review, and clear reasoning. If a student chooses the deeper
coding path, review whether the code supports the analysis, but do not award
extra credit merely for writing more of it.
